# Gibiverse

Academic course material-sharing and Q&A platform for university students.

## Language

### Academic hierarchy

**University**:
An institution of higher education. The top of the academic hierarchy.
_Avoid_: School, institution, college

**Department**:
An internal academic unit within a university that groups related courses. Used as a browsing and filtering dimension in the Course Directory, while quick upload and question pickers use University directly.
_Avoid_: Faculty, school, division

**Course**:
A single class or subject offered by a department, identified by a code (e.g., CS201).
_Avoid_: Class, subject, module

### Content

**Course Material**:
A file uploaded by a student and tied to a course — past exams, lecture notes, slides, assignments, or lab materials. Identified by title, course, academic period, and course material type (without description).
_Avoid_: Resource, file, document, upload, content

**Course Material Type**:
A classification of what kind of academic material a course material is: exam, notes, slides, assignment, lab, or other.
_Avoid_: Resource type, category, kind

**Academic Period**:
The combined academic year and session (e.g., "2026/2027 semester 1", "2026/2027 semester 2", "2026/2027 summer") a course material is associated with.
_Avoid_: Term, quarter, separate year/semester

### People

**Student**:
A university learner using the platform. An authenticated user who can upload course materials, ask questions, post answers, and vote.
_Avoid_: User, member, account, profile

### Q&A

**Q&A**:
A question together with its answers and at most one accepted answer, treated as one unit of academic discussion for a course.
_Avoid_: Thread, post, discussion thread

**Question**:
A student's academic query tied to a course, with a title and body.
_Avoid_: Post, thread, topic

**Answer**:
A response to a question, posted by any student.
_Avoid_: Reply, comment, response

**Accepted Answer**:
The single answer the question author marks as best. At most one per question.
_Avoid_: Best answer, correct answer, solution

**Vote**:
An upvote (+1) or downvote (−1) cast by a student on a question or answer. One vote per student per target, togglable.
_Avoid_: Like, reaction, rating
